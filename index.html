Privacy Policy for ScanFlow Pro

📅 Effective Date: May 20, 2026 🔄 Last Updated: May 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ScanFlow Pro: Document Scanner & PDF Creator ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use our Android application. By using ScanFlow Pro, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Summary: ScanFlow Pro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All document processing happens entirely on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

ScanFlow Pro does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Account credentials — no login or registration required
  • Photos, documents, or scanned images uploaded to any server
  • Location data (GPS or network-based)
  • Device identifiers beyond what AdMob requires for ad delivery

All scanned files are saved locally on your device only, in Pictures/ScanFlowPro (images) or Download/ScanFlowPro (PDFs).

Permissions & Why We Use Them

CAMERA Camera Access

Used solely to capture document images through the device camera. Images are processed on-device only and are never transmitted to any server.

STORAGE Storage Access

Required on Android 9 (API 28) and below to write JPEG/PDF files to public folders. On Android 10 (API 29) and above, the app uses the MediaStore API — no storage permission is needed.

INTERNET Internet Access

Used exclusively by Google AdMob to serve advertisements. The App itself has no backend server and makes no network requests of its own.

Third-Party Services — Google AdMob

ScanFlow Pro is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may automatically collect certain data such as device advertising identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data in order to deliver and measure advertisements.

This data collection is governed entirely by Google's own Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

To opt out of personalized ads, visit Google's Ads Settings:
https://adssettings.google.com

Data Storage & Retention

ScanFlow Pro does not maintain any server-side data. Scanned files live solely on your device and remain under your full control. You can delete them at any time via the in-app Library screen or your device's file manager.

Children's Privacy

ScanFlow Pro is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum digital age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the App in a way that raises a privacy concern, please contact us using the details in Section 8.

Data Security

Because all document data is processed and stored entirely on-device, there is no risk of a server-side breach exposing your scans. We recommend enabling device-level security (screen lock, biometric authentication) to protect locally stored files.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in the App or applicable laws. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of ScanFlow Pro after any change constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
