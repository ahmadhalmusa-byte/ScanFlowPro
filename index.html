Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 20, 2026  |  Last Updated: May 20, 2026

ScanFlow Pro ("we", "our", or "the app") is a free mobile application for Android that lets users scan physical documents using their device camera, apply edits, and save results as JPEG images or PDF files. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

ScanFlow Pro does not:

  • Require account registration or login
  • Upload photos, scans, or documents to any server
  • Collect your name, email address, or any personal identifier
  • Track your location
  • Share your data with third parties (except as described in Section 3)

All scanned documents are stored locally on your device only (in Pictures/ScanFlowPro or Download/ScanFlowPro).

2. Permissions Used

CAMERA Camera Access

Used solely to capture document images. Images are processed locally and never transmitted to our servers.

STORAGE Storage Access

Required on Android 9 and below to save JPEG and PDF files to the device's public directories. On Android 10 and above, the app uses the MediaStore API which does not require a storage permission.

INTERNET Internet Access

Used exclusively to load advertisements served by Google AdMob. The app itself does not communicate with any backend server.

3. Third-Party Services – Google AdMob

ScanFlow Pro displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect and use data such as device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction events to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.

Google's use of data is governed by their own Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting https://adssettings.google.com.

4. Data Retention

Since ScanFlow Pro does not collect personal data, there is no data for us to retain or delete. Scanned files remain on your device and can be removed at any time through the app's Library screen or your device's file manager.

5. Children's Privacy

ScanFlow Pro is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Security

Because all document data is processed and stored entirely on-device, there is no risk of a server-side data breach affecting your scans. We recommend using device-level security (PIN, fingerprint) to protect your stored files.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
